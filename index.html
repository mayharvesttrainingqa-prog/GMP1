GMP AND LOOSE ITEM CONTROL MONITORING

Personnel entry inspection • Loose item declaration • Independent verification

1. Incoming Personnel Registration

Time In is filled with the current time and can be adjusted. Time Out is logged later from Monitoring Records once the person has been allowed in.

2. GMP Personnel Entry Checklist

Tick every item that is satisfied. Leave an item unticked if it is not met; the reviewer will see it flagged.

3. Loose Item Declaration

Declare all loose items carried into the production or warehouse building, including approved tools, stationery, personal items, and other objects.

Leave blank if no loose items are being brought in.
